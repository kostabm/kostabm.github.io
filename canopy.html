You're an ape with no legs worth mentioning. Plant your hands on anything and shove yourself around the ruins: climb, fling and swing. Don't get caught by the slimed apes.

When a hand touches something it grabs on. Move the mouse while holding to push your body around that hand, then let go to fling.
Tip: grab the ground ahead, flick the mouse down, let go. Alternate hands to run fast. Grab a wall, look down, let go to climb.
